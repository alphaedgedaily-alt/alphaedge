Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy describes how the Alpha Edge Daily publishing tool ("the App") handles information. The App is an internal tool used solely by the owner of the Alpha Edge Daily YouTube channel to upload and manage videos on that channel.

What the App accesses

The App uses Google OAuth and the YouTube API Services. When the channel owner signs in, the App requests permission to upload videos, set video details (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, visibility, and scheduling), and read basic channel and video analytics for the owner's own channel.

How information is used

Information is used only to publish and manage videos on the owner's own channel and to review that channel's performance. The App does not access, collect, or store data about other YouTube users or viewers.

Storage and security

OAuth access tokens are stored securely and used only by the App to perform the actions above. No personal data is sold, shared with, or disclosed to third parties.

Google and YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. Stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked or upon request.
